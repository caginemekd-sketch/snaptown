The short version. SnapTown has no accounts, no sign-in, and no servers of its own. We never ask for your name, email address or phone number. Your scores and progress stay on your device. The app does show ads and offers in-app purchases, and for those two things Google receives some data about your device. Details below.

1. Information stored on your device

The following is saved locally by the app and is never transmitted to us or to anyone else:

  • Your best score and your current game board
  • Your in-game gold balance
  • Your chosen language
  • Which coupon codes you have already redeemed
  • Whether you own the ad-free purchase, the Platinum Pack, or an active subscription
  • Whether you have seen the tutorial and the rating prompt

This data lives only in the app's own storage. Uninstalling SnapTown deletes all of it. We have no copy and no way to retrieve it.

2. Information collected by Google

SnapTown uses three Google services. Each collects data under Google's own privacy policy, not ours.

Google AdMob (advertising)

To show ads, the Google Mobile Ads SDK may collect and process:

  • Your device's advertising ID (a resettable identifier)
  • Device information such as model, operating system version and language
  • Your IP address, and the approximate region derived from it
  • Ad interactions ÔÇö which ads were shown, viewed or tapped

The app declares the com.google.android.gms.permission.AD_ID permission for this purpose. Ads may be personalised or non-personalised depending on your settings and your region. See How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services.

Google Play Billing (purchases)

If you buy the ad-free upgrade, the Platinum Pack, or a subscription, the transaction is handled entirely by Google Play. The app receives only a confirmation that a purchase is active. Your card number, billing address and payment details never reach the app and are never visible to us.

Google Analytics for Firebase

SnapTown uses Google Analytics for Firebase to understand how the game is played in aggregate. It collects:

  • App opens, session length and how often you come back
  • An app instance identifier that Google generates for this installation
  • Device model, operating system version, country and language

This is aggregate, statistical information. It is not linked to your name or email address, and we cannot identify an individual player from it. We use it to decide what to fix and what to build next. See Privacy and Security in Firebase.

3. What we do not do

  • We do not run any server, and the app sends no data to us
  • We use no crash reporting SDK and no social media SDK
  • We do not collect your name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos or files
  • We do not access your precise location, camera or microphone
  • We do not sell or share personal information with data brokers

SnapTown is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided information through the app, contact us at the address below and we will act on it.

7. If you are in the European Economic Area, the UK or Switzerland

Under the GDPR you have the right to access, correct, delete, restrict or object to the processing of your personal data, and the right to data portability. Because we hold no personal data ourselves, requests about advertising data should be directed to Google, which acts as the controller for that processing. You may also lodge a complaint with your national data protection authority.

8. If you are in California

Under the CCPA/CPRA you have the right to know what personal information is collected, to request its deletion, and to opt out of its sale or sharing. We do not sell or share personal information. Advertising data is handled by Google; see Google's privacy policy for the controls available to you.

9. Data retention

We hold no personal data ourselves. Analytics data is stored by Google on our behalf and is retained for up to 14 months. Data held on your device is kept until you uninstall the app or clear its storage. Data collected by Google is retained according to Google's own retention schedules.

10. Security

Because SnapTown stores data only on your device and transmits nothing to us, there is no account of yours for us to lose. Purchase verification is handled by Google Play over an encrypted connection.

11. Changes to this policy
